Ugali: Bring 3 cups of water to a boil in a pot. Gradually add 2 cups of maize flour while stirring vigorously with a wooden spoon (mwiko) to break up lumps. Pound the dough against the sides of the pot until thick and firm, then cover and let it steam on low heat for 5 minutes before shaping.
Nyama Choma: Cut 1 kg of beef or goat meat into large chunks and rub generously with salt, oil, and optional minced garlic. Grill over hot charcoal or roast in an oven at 200°C for 30–45 minutes, turning occasionally until browned. Slice into bite-sized pieces.
Kachumbari: Finely dice 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, 1 chili, and fresh coriander. Soak the chopped onions in salted water for 5 minutes, then rinse and drain to remove the sharp bite. Mix all ingredients in a bowl with a pinch of salt and fresh lemon juice.
Makange: Boil 500g of cubed meat with garlic, ginger, and salt until tender. In a pan, sauté 1 onion and 1 bell pepper, add the boiled meat to brown, then stir in 2 chopped tomatoes, 1 tbsp tomato paste, and a splash of soy sauce. Simmer for 7 minutes until thick.
