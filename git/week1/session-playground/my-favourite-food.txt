Wali wa Nazi (Coconut Rice): Boil 2 cups of rice in 3 cups of light coconut milk with a pinch of salt and cardamom. When the liquid drops, pour 1 cup of heavy coconut cream on top, cover, and steam on low heat for 20 minutes.
Maharage ya Nazi (Coconut Beans): Simmer 2 cups of boiled kidney beans with onions, tomatoes, garlic, curry powder, and 2 cups of light coconut milk for 10 minutes. Stir in 1/2 cup of heavy coconut cream and cook for 2 minutes.
Samaki wa Nazi (Coconut Fish): Fry seasoned fish pieces until golden and set aside. Cook onions, tomatoes, and curry powder, then pour in 1.5 cups of coconut milk. Add the fish back and simmer for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
Kisamvu (Cassava Leaves): Boil pounded cassava leaves until completely tender. Add chopped onions, tomatoes, and 1 cup of heavy coconut cream, then simmer on low heat until creamy and dry.
